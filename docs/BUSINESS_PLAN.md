### Mission Statement
Empower businesses of all sizes to automate their work with intelligent AI agents that actually get things done.

### Problem
Businesses waste thousands of hours on repetitive tasks that could be automated:
- Sales teams manually score leads
- HR spends hours screening resumes
- Support teams answer the same questions repeatedly
- Marketing creates content from scratch every time

### Solution
A multi-tenant AI platform with pre-built agents that:
- Automate common business tasks
- Work across multiple channels (Telegram, Slack, Email)
- Scale from solo entrepreneurs to enterprises
- Cost fraction of hiring humans

### Business Model
SaaS subscription with usage-based pricing:
- Free tier: 10K tokens/month
- Starter: $29/month (100K tokens)
- Pro: $99/month (500K tokens)
- Enterprise: $499/month (unlimited)

## Market Analysis

- **AI Agents Market:** $5B (2026), growing to $50B by 2030

### Target Market

- **Size:** 10-500 employees

- **Pain Points:** Limited staff, repetitive tasks, cost pressure
- **Willingness to Pay:** $100-$500/month for automation

#### Secondary: Enterprise
- **Size:** 500+ employees
- **Use Cases:** Department-specific automation
- **Willingness to Pay:** $5K-$50K/month

#### Tertiary: Solopreneurs
- **Size:** 1-10 people
- **Use Cases:** Personal productivity
- **Willingness to Pay:** $0-$50/month

### Market Trends
1. **AI Adoption:** 70% of businesses plan to adopt AI by 2027
2. **Remote Work:** Increased need for automation
3. **Cost Pressure:** Businesses cutting costs post-pandemic
4. **Chat Interfaces:** Telegram/Slack already widely used

#### Strengths

#### Weaknesses

- ❌ Limited team (founder-only initially)
- ❌ No enterprise features yet
- ❌ Dependent on third-party AI (OpenRouter)

#### Opportunities

#### Threats
